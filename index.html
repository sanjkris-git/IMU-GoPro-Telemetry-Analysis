GoPro IMU Lap Analysis

Purdue Grand Prix Track · KT-100 karts · HERO13 Black · 13:38 clip · 200 Hz accelerometer + gyro
No GPS fix in this clip (lat/lon are all zeros in both .txt files and the .bin), so position isn’t recorded. The layout is the real Purdue Grand Prix circuit from the open-source Triton-AI/PurdueGrandPrixTrack scene (Apache 2.0); its centerline measures m against the 434 m published on Wikipedia. The camera’s gyro turn pattern is matched to that layout to place the kart on the track, and speed is derived from that. S/F is set under the pedestrian bridge, where the GoPro’s exposure also shows the kart entering shade each lap. Treat speed as an estimate (solid in corners, interpolated on straights); accelerometer g-forces read about 25% below speed² × curvature, so they are probably conservative.
Hover or tap the map or any chart to read values along the lap.
